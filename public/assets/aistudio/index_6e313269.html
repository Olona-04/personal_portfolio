AgriBot: AI Chatbot for Small-Scale Farmers

A chatbot that lets farmers describe a crop problem in everyday language. It asks up to three focused questions, then gives a likely cause (not a diagnosis) with prevention tips, and refers serious cases to an advisor. Built by Transformers Group, a team of 5.

MoodSpark: AI Sentiment Analysis

A mood and sentiment app that turns words into insights. It analyses single texts and batches of comments with two engines that run in the browser, VADER and a transformer-style model, and has a daily mood journal that checks how closely your words match your mood. Built with React and TypeScript.

ReactTypeScriptViteTailwind CSSVADERNLP
Live demo

Content Generator & Social Publisher

An AI app that makes text, code or images from a prompt, lets you add a caption, then posts to your socials. It also suggests hashtags and has a prompt library, tone options and a colour palette picker. The frontend is React with TypeScript, with a Node/Express backend.
